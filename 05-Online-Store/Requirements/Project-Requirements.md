# Project 5: Online Store Management System

## Project Requirements

### 1. Product Catalog Management:
* Stores information about products available for sale.
* Attributes include: Product Name, Description, Price, Quantity In Stock, Images, and other relevant details.

### 2. Customer Information:
* Stores information about registered customers.
* Attributes include: Customer Name, Contact Details, Shipping Address, and Login Credentials.

### 3. Order Information:
* Stores details about customer orders: Order Number, Customer Details, Order Date/Time, Purchased Products, Quantities, Prices, Shipping Method, and Order Status.
* Order Statuses:
  * Pending
  * Processing
  * Shipped
  * Delivered
  * Cancelled
  * Refunded

### 4. Payment Transaction:
* Tracks financial transactions: Transaction ID, Customer Details, Payment Amount, Payment Method, and Timestamp.

### 5. Shipping:
* Tracks logistics and deliveries: Order ID, Shipping Carrier Details, Tracking Number, Shipping Status, Estimated Delivery Date, Actual Delivery Date, and Shipping Notes.
* Shipping Statuses:
  1. Processing
  2. Out for Delivery
  3. Delivered
  4. Return to Sender
  5. On Hold
  6. Delayed
  7. Lost

### 6. Reviews and Ratings:
* Records customer feedback on products: Product ID, Customer ID, Review Text, Rating Score (1 to 5), and Timestamps.
